Acceptable Use Policy

Effective Date: May 25, 2026 | Last Updated: May 25, 2026

1. Introduction

This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") governs your use of Veklom's governed AI marketplace, compute routing platform, and related services ("Services"). By accessing or using the Services, you agree to comply with this AUP. Veklom reserves the right to suspend or terminate access for violations.

2. Prohibited Content

You must not use the Services to generate, process, publish, or distribute content that is illegal or violates these standards. Specifically, you may not post or process content that:

  • Facilitates unlawful gambling
  • Facilitates sexually related services, including prostitution or escort services
  • Facilitates the sale of narcotics, regulated goods, or controlled substances
  • Contains child exploitation content, including images or depictions of minors in the nude or suggestive poses
  • Facilitates or enables fundraising or support for terrorist organizations
  • Threatens violence, physical harm, or harassment against individuals or groups
  • Infringes on copyrights, trademarks, patents, or other intellectual property rights (we respond to DMCA takedown requests)
  • Distributes scams, malware, phishing, or other fraudulent and deceptive content
  • Contains non-consensual images of other people or impersonates others
  • Contains sensitive or legal adult content that is not properly labeled as NSFW (Not Safe For Work)

3. Prohibited Activities

You must not engage in activities that:

  • Attempt to bypass security controls or authentication mechanisms
  • Probe, scan, or test the vulnerability of any system or network
  • Inject malicious code, malware, or harmful scripts
  • Engage in denial-of-service attacks or abuse API rate limits
  • Reverse engineer, decompile, or attempt to extract source code
  • Use automated tools to scrape or harvest data without authorization
  • Create accounts or use the Services through false identities
  • Interfere with or disrupt the Services or servers

4. AI-Specific Rules

When using AI models through Veklom:

  • Do not attempt to jailbreak, bypass safety filters, or exploit model vulnerabilities
  • Do not use prompt injection techniques to override system instructions
  • Do not generate content designed to evade content moderation systems
  • Do not use AI to generate synthetic media for deception or fraud
  • Respect model usage limits and fair use policies
  • Do not attempt to extract model weights or training data

5. Marketplace and Vendor Rules

Vendors on the Veklom Marketplace must:

  • Provide accurate descriptions of listings and capabilities
  • Honor all stated pricing and service level agreements
  • Comply with all applicable laws and regulations
  • Not use the Marketplace to distribute malicious software or services
  • Maintain the security and integrity of their integrations

6. API Usage Rules

When using Veklom APIs:

  • Authenticate using valid API keys and do not share credentials
  • Respect rate limits and implement proper backoff strategies
  • Do not cache or redistribute API responses beyond intended use
  • Use API endpoints only for their documented purposes
  • Implement proper error handling and logging

7. Enforcement and Content Moderation Framework

Veklom monitors for violations of this AUP through automated reporting, filters, and manual review. We utilize a content moderation framework guided by local laws and the spirit of the First Amendment to the United States Constitution to determine what legal speech is rendered in our applications:

  1. Local Jurisdictional Legality: If content is determined to be illegal in a specific jurisdiction in which we operate, we will remove or restrict it in that jurisdiction.
  2. First Amendment Protections: In the United States, we generally allow content to be rendered if it is protected under the First Amendment. Any content that is unprotected (such as child exploitation, direct threats of violence, or criminal facilitation) will be removed globally.
  3. App Store and Platform Compliance: If partner app stores or distribution channels require content removal to comply with their guidelines, we will remove the content globally or locally as required.

NSFW and Sensitive Content: While Veklom allows legal adult and sensitive content where permitted by law, users must label such posts as NSFW. Failure to do so will result in content removal or account restriction.

Decentralized Persistence Disclaimer: Veklom interfaces with decentralized protocols (such as Farcaster and XMTP). If content is flagged and removed under this AUP, it will be hidden or filtered from the Veklom client interface. However, because these protocols are decentralized, the content will continue to persist on the Farcaster network, XMTP network, or underlying public blockchain, and Veklom has no capability to delete it from those external environments.

Violations of this AUP may result in:

  • Warning notices and requests for corrective action
  • Temporary or permanent suspension of account access
  • Removal of content or listings from the Veklom UI and Marketplace
  • Termination of service agreements
  • Referral to law enforcement for illegal activities
