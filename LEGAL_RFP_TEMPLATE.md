# Legal RFP Template: Fixed-Fee Contract Review

Use this template to solicit firm-fixed price quotes from legal counsel for contract reviews, forcing a clear SLA and bounded cost.

---

**Subject:** Fixed‑fee quote to redline attached contract (48–72h SLA)

Hi [Name/Team],

I’m requesting a firm‑fixed fee quote for a **single contract review** (PDF attached).

**Scope & goal**

* **Scope:** One contract (see attached PDF).
* **Goal:**

  1. Redline the contract,
  2. Provide a **1‑page risk memo** (top risks, must‑fix items, nice‑to‑have edits),
  3. Up to **2 rounds of comments** after the initial delivery.

**Deliverables**

* Edited PDF (tracked changes / redlines clearly visible).
* 1‑page risk memo (bullets are fine).

**SLA**

* Delivery within **48–72 business hours** of engagement start.

**Pricing**

* Please provide a **single firm‑fixed total** (USD) covering the full scope above.
* List any **explicit exclusions** (if any) so we can confirm fit.

**Acceptance & start**

* We’ll accept by **e‑signature** and pay a **retainer ≤ $250** to start (applied to the fixed fee).

Please reply with: total fixed fee, exclusions (if any), and your earliest start time.
If acceptable, I’ll countersign and fund immediately.

Thanks,
[Your Name]
[Title / Company]
[Phone] | [Email]

---

## Pre‑send checklist:

* [ ] Attach the PDF.
* [ ] Fill in names, your details, and preferred start date.
* [ ] If you have non‑negotiables, add a short line under “Goal” (e.g., “Indemnity must be mutual; data processing must meet [X] standard”).
